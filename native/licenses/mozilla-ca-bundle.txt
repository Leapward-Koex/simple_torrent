Mozilla CA certificate bundle notice
====================================

The Apple artifacts embed the Mozilla CA certificate store converted to PEM
by curl's CA Extract service. The pinned source form is:

  Version: 2026-08-13
  URL: https://curl.se/ca/cacert-2026-08-13.pem
  SHA-256: f66dff1bdf8f96060b8177976f8b7d9254bc89bc4db933d769f7384d28480bc9
  License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0)
  License text: https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/

The unmodified PEM source can be obtained from the URL above and its checksum
is enforced by the native builder before it is embedded.
